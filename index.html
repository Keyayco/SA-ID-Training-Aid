Authentic Smart ID

Front (Authentic)

Back (Authentic)

Authentic Features

  • Color: Blue and white design
  • Material: Polycarbonate plastic
  • Chip: Visible gold-colored chip
  • Hologram: 3D holographic element
  • Microprinting: Tiny text (magnification needed)
  • UV Features: Visible under UV light
  • Watermark: Faint background watermark

Fake Smart ID

Common Fake Methods

  • Chip cloning
  • Photo replacement
  • Data manipulation
  • Poor printing
  • Missing holograms
  • Chip issues

Common Indicators

Misaligned or non-functional chips, poor printing, missing or blurry holograms, incorrect color gradients, and low-quality photographs.

Damaged Smart ID

Common Damage Types

  • Cracked or broken card
  • Worn-out chip
  • Faded or scratched surface
  • Peeling layers (delamination)
  • Water or heat damage

Risk Indicators

  • ⚠️ Chip not readable
  • ⚠️ Photo unclear or distorted
  • ⚠️ Missing security features
  • ⚠️ Data partially unreadable

Important: A damaged ID is not automatically fraudulent, but must be verified with extra caution.

Red Flags

  • 🚩 Misaligned or damaged chip
  • 🚩 Poor color reproduction
  • 🚩 Missing 3D hologram
  • 🚩 Scratches or damage
  • 🚩 Incorrect font or spacing
  • 🚩 Faded information
  • 🚩 Chip not responding
  • 🚩 Altered or pasted photo

Verification Procedure

Step 1: Visual Inspection

  • Check overall appearance
  • Look for holograms and watermarks
  • Verify text clarity
  • Check photo quality

Step 2: Physical Examination

  • Feel material texture
  • Check for tampering
  • Inspect edges
  • Look for raised elements

Step 3: Electronic Verification

  • Test chip functionality
  • Scan barcode
  • Match stored data
  • Check encryption

Step 4: Information Verification

  • Cross-check databases
  • Verify ID format
  • Check validity dates
  • Confirm personal info

Authentic Green ID Book

Key Features to Verify

  • Barcode: Must match printed ID number
  • Lamination: High-quality, clear and firmly sealed
  • Watermarks: SA coat of arms visible under light
  • Font: Consistent spacing and alignment
  • Photo: Clear and properly embedded

Fake Green ID Book

Common Fake Methods

  • Photo substitution
  • Information alteration (name, DOB, ID number)
  • Cloned documents using stolen data
  • Low-quality materials replacing original lamination
  • Missing or poorly replicated security features

Common Manipulations

Altered dates, replaced photos, mismatched ID numbers, and inconsistent document details are typical fraud indicators.

Damaged Green ID Book

Common Damage Types

  • Torn or frayed pages
  • Peeling or bubbled lamination
  • Water-stained or discolored pages
  • Faded photo or text
  • Bent spine or loose binding
  • Barcode worn or unreadable

Risk Indicators

  • ⚠️ Barcode cannot be scanned
  • ⚠️ Photo is obscured or partially missing
  • ⚠️ ID number partially illegible
  • ⚠️ Lamination peeling around photo area (possible tampering)
  • ⚠️ Watermarks no longer visible

Important: Peeling lamination around the photo is a key fraud indicator — escalate immediately. General wear is not automatically fraudulent but requires closer inspection.

Red Flags

  • 🚩 Barcode does not match ID number
  • 🚩 Peeling or cloudy lamination
  • 🚩 Missing or unclear watermarks
  • 🚩 Inconsistent font or spacing
  • 🚩 Tampered edges or glued photo
  • 🚩 Blurry or low-quality image

Verification Procedure

Step 1: Visual Inspection

  • Check color, layout, and print quality
  • Inspect lamination and photo placement
  • Look for watermarks under light

Step 2: Barcode Verification

  • Scan barcode using a reader or mobile app
  • Ensure barcode data matches printed ID number

Step 3: Cross-Verification

  • Compare with other documents (passport, license)
  • Check for mismatched personal details

Step 4: Official Verification

  • Verify with Department of Home Affairs (DHA)
  • Use online services or contact DHA

Step 5: Advanced Checks

  • Use UV light or magnification tools
  • Check for hidden security features

Note: If fraud is suspected, report to SAPS or DHA.

Authentic South African Passport

Data Page Features

  • Material: Polycarbonate data page
  • Laser Engraving: Photo and text embedded (not printed)
  • OVI Ink: "RSA" changes color when tilted
  • Latent Image: Hidden RSA appears at angle
  • Tactile Print: Raised intaglio texture

Physical Construction

  • Laser-perforated passport number on pages
  • Ghost image on final visa page
  • White stitching (glows under UV)
  • Chemically sensitized paper

MRZ (Machine Readable Zone)

  • Starts with P<ZAF
  • Contains encoded personal data
  • Includes check digits for validation

Fake Passport Methods

Common Fraud Techniques

  • Photo substitution or page replacement
  • Fake laminate or printed data pages
  • MRZ manipulation without valid check digits
  • Forged stamps or visas
  • Missing laser engraving

Advanced Fraud

  • Cloned passport data
  • Fake perforation patterns
  • Imitated UV features

Damaged Passport

Common Damage Types

  • Water damage — swollen, warped, or stained pages
  • Torn or detached pages
  • Worn or faded data page
  • Bent spine or broken binding
  • MRZ scratched or partially unreadable
  • Photo area faded or discolored

Risk Indicators

  • ⚠️ MRZ cannot be scanned or read
  • ⚠️ Data page shows signs of delamination
  • ⚠️ Photo is unclear or distorted
  • ⚠️ OVI ink color shift cannot be verified
  • ⚠️ Perforated passport number missing or damaged

Important: Even a damaged passport must retain readable MRZ and a recognisable photo for verification. Delamination on the data page is a serious fraud flag — escalate immediately.

Red Flags

  • 🚩 Printed (not engraved) photo or text
  • 🚩 No color shift in OVI ink
  • 🚩 Missing or incorrect MRZ format
  • 🚩 MRZ does not match visible data
  • 🚩 No perforated passport number
  • 🚩 Loose or damaged stitching
  • 🚩 Poor-quality paper or printing
  • 🚩 Fake or inconsistent stamps

Verification Procedure

Step 1: Visual Inspection

  • Check data page material and engraving
  • Look for OVI color shift
  • Inspect photo and text clarity

Step 2: MRZ Verification

  • Check format (P<ZAF)
  • Scan MRZ using reader
  • Validate check digits

Step 3: Physical Inspection

  • Check stitching and binding
  • Inspect perforations
  • Use UV light for hidden features

Step 4: Database Verification

  • Verify via Department of Home Affairs
  • Use authorized services (VerifyNow, MIE)
  • Cross-check identity details

Step 5: Biometric / Advanced

  • Perform face matching (if available)
  • Check against national databases

Note: Report suspected fraud to authorities.

Temporary Identity Certificate (TIC)

Official Features

  • Document Type: A4 paper certificate
  • DHA Stamp: Original wet-ink stamp
  • Photographs: Two identical colour photos
  • Signature: Issuing officer must sign
  • ID Number: Standard 13-digit SA format

Fake Temporary ID Methods

Common Fraud Techniques

  • Photocopied or fake DHA stamp
  • Photo replacement (glued or lifted)
  • Forged signature
  • Expired document reused
  • Fake or invalid ID number

Damaged Temporary ID Certificate

Common Damage Types

  • Torn, folded, or crumpled paper
  • Water-stained or faded text
  • Smudged ink on stamp or signature
  • Detached or missing photo
  • Illegible ID number due to wear

Risk Indicators

  • ⚠️ DHA stamp unreadable or partially missing
  • ⚠️ Signature absent or smeared beyond recognition
  • ⚠️ Photo loose or clearly re-attached
  • ⚠️ Key fields (ID number, name, DOB) illegible
  • ⚠️ Expiry date unreadable

Important: TICs are already high-risk documents. Any damage that obscures the stamp, signature, photo, or ID number must result in immediate escalation. Do not accept a damaged TIC without supervisor approval.

Red Flags

  • 🚩 Missing or copied DHA stamp
  • 🚩 Expired certificate
  • 🚩 Photo lifting or glue marks
  • 🚩 No issuing officer signature
  • 🚩 Incorrect ID number format
  • 🚩 Barcode mismatch (if present)
  • 🚩 Damaged or altered paper

Verification Procedure

Step 1: Visual Inspection

  • Check stamp authenticity (wet ink)
  • Inspect photo attachment
  • Confirm signature present

Step 2: Expiry Check

  • Ensure document is within validity (≈ 2 months)
  • Reject expired TICs

Step 3: ID Number Logic

  • Verify 13-digit format (YYMMDDSSSSCAZ)
  • Check gender range (0000–4999 / 5000–9999)
  • Validate checksum (Luhn algorithm)

Step 4: Barcode / Data Check

  • Scan barcode (if available)
  • Match encoded ID with printed number

Step 5: Database Verification

  • Verify via DHA or authorized services
  • Confirm ID exists and holder status is valid

Note: TICs are high-risk documents. Always escalate if uncertain.

Common Fraud Methods

1. Photo Substitution (Photo Swap)

Replacing the original photo with another person's image.

  • Common in Green ID Books
  • Often involves lifting laminate

2. Information Alteration

Changing personal details such as name, ID number, or date of birth.

  • Erasing or covering original text
  • Reprinting or overwriting details

3. Document Cloning

Creating a duplicate using stolen identity data.

  • Scanned and reprinted documents
  • Missing real security features

4. Chip Tampering

Altering or replacing microchips in smart documents.

  • Reprogrammed chips
  • Data mismatch with printed info

5. Identity Theft

Using another person's identity to create documents.

  • Stolen personal data
  • Used in financial fraud

6. Barcode Tampering

Replacing or modifying barcode data.

  • Fake barcode overlays
  • Mismatch with printed ID

7. Synthetic Identity Fraud

Combining real and fake information to create a new identity.

  • Real ID number + fake name
  • Passes basic checks

8. Identity Selling (Syndicates)

Real identities used by other individuals.

  • Often involves corruption
  • Biometric/photo mismatch

Document Verification Techniques

👁️ Visual Inspection

  • Check overall appearance and color
  • Look for holograms and watermarks
  • Verify text clarity and alignment
  • Check photo quality and placement
  • Inspect microprinting

🔍 Physical Examination

  • Feel material texture and weight
  • Check for tampering or glue marks
  • Inspect edges and corners
  • Look for raised or embossed elements
  • Check stitching and binding

💻 Electronic Verification

  • Test chip functionality
  • Scan barcodes
  • Verify stored data
  • Check encryption/security

📋 Information Verification

  • Cross-check with government databases
  • Validate ID number format
  • Check expiry dates
  • Confirm personal information

🧠 Logic Checks

ID Number Structure

  • Validate ID number using Luhn algorithm
  • Check gender digits (0000–4999 / 5000–9999)
  • Match age with appearance
  • Ensure data consistency across document

What To Do If Fraud Is Suspected

  1. Do not return the document
  2. Report to your supervisor immediately
  3. Document all findings
  4. Escalate internally
  5. Contact authorities if required

Report fraud to:

  • South African Police Service (SAPS)
  • Department of Home Affairs

📤 Submission Standards

Before submitting an application, ensure the ID document and selfie meet all requirements below. Applications that do not meet these standards may be delayed, require corrections, or be rejected.

🤳

Customer Selfie

  • Customer must face the camera directly
  • Eyes must be open and visible
  • Image must be clear and in focus
  • Hairline and ears must be visible
  • Customer must be wearing appropriate clothing
  • Customer must be physically present in-store
  • Selfie must be taken live
✅ Acceptable if the customer's face clearly matches the ID document.
🪪

ID Document

  • Entire document must be visible
  • No corners or pages may be cut off
  • All text must be readable
  • ID number must be clearly visible
  • No glare, reflections, or flash
  • No objects may cover the document
  • Image must be sharp and in focus
  • Document must be original and physically present
  • Document must not be expired
✅ Ensure all information can be read without zooming.
📄

Accepted Documents

  • South African Smart ID Card
  • South African Green ID Book
  • Valid South African Temporary ID
❌ Do not submit driver's licences or foreign IDs.
⚠️

Fraud Prevention Checks

  • Customer must match the ID photo
  • Document must appear genuine
  • Selfie must appear genuine
  • No screenshots or photos of photos
  • No edited, filtered, or altered images
  • Customer must meet minimum age requirements
